Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

We at TwinApp Studio are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices:

1. Data Collection: The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. All operations run locally on your device.

2. Battery Permissions: Battery status and level APIs are accessed locally solely to trigger audio alerts when charging targets are met to preserve battery lifespan.

3. Ads & Tracking: This app is completely ad-free and contains no third-party user tracking mechanisms.

4. Contact Us: For any questions regarding privacy, please contact TwinApp Studio support.
