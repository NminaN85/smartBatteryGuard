Privacy Policy

Smart Battery Guard | Developed by TwinApp Studio

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Smart Battery Guard. We at TwinApp Studio respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Usage

Our application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, usage statistics, or location data. All operations regarding battery level checking and temperature monitoring are performed locally using standard device browser or system APIs.

3. Device Permissions

Smart Battery Guard accesses local battery status APIs solely for the purpose of alerting you when your charging target is reached to help prolong battery lifespan. No data obtained from these permissions is transmitted outside your device.

4. Third-Party Services and Ads

The application is completely ad-free and contains no third-party trackers, analytics frameworks, or monetization software development kits (SDKs) that gather user information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address or knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, no children's data is ever stored.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.

7. Contact Us
